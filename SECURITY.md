# Security Policy

This policy applies to every ObolNetwork repository that does not have its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a security problem.

Email **security@obol.tech** with:

- the affected repository, component and version
- steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- the impact you expect

Send reports in plain text, with any proof of concept attached. We aim to reply within 48 hours.

## Bug bounty

Valid reports in scope can earn a reward. Scope, rules and rewards are in the [Obol Bug Bounty Program](https://docs.obol.org/advanced-and-troubleshooting/security/bug-bounty).
